YOU'RE RUNNING THE SHOW

Bring the team together.

Create a room and share the invite link. Put your host screen on the big screen or share it on a call. Everyone answers separately on their own device.

Choose 1–60 questions and 5–120 seconds per answer in your lobby. Every game starts with the New Hampshire hometown map. Round 2 is a shuffled nine-square photo puzzle with its own 30-second limit; the first finisher wins that round. Round 3 introduces Alex’s dog. Shorter games stop at your chosen count. The 60-question bank has 33 Rochester, RIT, Manchester, and New Hampshire rounds, with just three music-and-brand household questions. The default 20-question game includes all seven photo rounds: the puzzle, the dog, the bike, the watch, two more designs, and a Europe location mystery. Map guesses appear at time-up with animated lines, names, avatars, and distances in miles. Themes are mixed apart. Photos include source credits.
